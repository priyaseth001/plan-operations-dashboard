Multi-Year Automation Summary: Across FY25 and FY26, LOA returns remained locked at ~25.0 to 25.8 days due to sequential dependencies where plan release awaited quota submission. In FY27, automated decoupled issuance reduced average turnaround to 11.1 days (-55.6% YoY), successfully absorbing a +56.5% surge in payee volume without ticket backlogs or quality issues.
1. Replan Quality & Rework Drop

Record-Low Replan Rate Reached (7.8%)

Overall replan rate fell from 10.7% to 7.8% (-26.6% YoY), with total replan counts dropping from 3,700 in FY26 to 3,400 YTD. The primary catalyst was a massive 75.0% reduction in Design Changes (from 800 down to 200), proving that upstream plan design governance successfully eliminated major rework cycles.

2. Cycle Time Anatomy & Platform Holds

Steady State Cycle Times Held Flat (31.6 Days)

While overall turnaround reached 34.8 days (+56.8%) due to mass G4G cycles (44.9d), steady state events (31.6d) remained essentially flat (+2.6% / +0.8d). These turnaround times were influenced by batched delivery schedules and manual holds from rolling out the new delivery platform. This workflow was automated in September, setting up cycle time reductions forward.

3. Targeted Automation Success

LOA Turnaround Slashed by 55.6% (11.1 Days)

LOA return cycle times dropped from 25.0 days in FY26 to 11.1 days YTD across 1,321 processed plans. The FY27 workflow automation eliminated quota submission dependencies for LOA returns, demonstrating the operational blueprint for streamlining lifecycle events.

4. Case Operations & Enablement Inquiries

Case Increase Driven by Adoption & Enablement

Case volume increased to 1,780 (+21.1% YoY) with a 14-day average resolution age. The volume surge was concentrated in enablement, plan status requests, platform access, and plan acknowledgement. Core transactional disputes around Territory, LOA, and Transfer/Promotions (TI) decreased.
